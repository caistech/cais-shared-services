"Agentic team" means at least four quite different things, and most of the time two people are describing different ones to each other and agreeing.

A swarm — AI helpers created to do one job, working side by side, gone when it's finished. This is the version most engineers mean, and it genuinely works.

Role-play — a Researcher, a Writer, a Critic. Usually one AI wearing different hats, all sharing exactly the same access to everything. The job titles are instructions, not restrictions.

A fleet — permanent assistants living inside a big platform you already pay for. They work because the platform was already there and had already decided who is allowed to see what.

Or a workflow — ordinary software doing most of the work by following rules, with AI only where something genuinely needs judgement.

Four quite different machines, one phrase, and almost nothing in common between them.

What separates them isn't how many there are or how clever any one of them is. It's whether any of them can be stopped by something other than being asked nicely.

So: ask which of the four you are being sold, and ask them to say it in those terms.

It is not a gotcha. A good vendor will answer it in a sentence and be pleased you asked. In my experience the question produces a much longer pause than it should — and the length of that pause is most of what you needed to know.
