Just Ship It. It'll be Fine. №6

Eight things broken. Four shipped that morning, by someone who checked all four.

It had been a good morning.

Every change verified, every check green, every one of them reported honestly.

Nothing skipped. Nobody rushing for the door.

Then a tester walked the product and came back with eight problems. Four of them hours old.

So my first question was: what did we miss?

Because on paper, nothing.

What we missed is that all the checking asked "did my change take effect" — and the answer was yes, every time.

Six of the eight weren't in anything that had changed.

They sat at the joins. A header added without noticing the layout already drew one. A price corrected on the checkout page while the page immediately before it still read "+ GST + GST".

We'd fixed the exact thing the report named and left the same thing alive two screens over.

Three separate times in one day.

And yet nobody did anything wrong. Every change was verified, and every verification was honest.

The obvious fix is to check more carefully.

But it was already careful. It was just aimed at the diff.

Verifying your change tells you what you wrote. It tells you nothing about what the customer gets.

So the unit is the whole path someone walks now, including the parts nobody touched. And nothing closes until we've gone looking for the same mistake everywhere else.

I wouldn't call this solved.

We're getting caught out in new places rather than the same ones. Which is progress, and isn't the same thing.
